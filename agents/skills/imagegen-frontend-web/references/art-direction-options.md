# Art Direction Options

Use these as a vocabulary for choosing a direction from the brief. Select only relevant options.

## Theme

- restrained editorial;
- cinematic product story;
- warm humanist;
- technical precision;
- playful geometric;
- archival or print-inspired;
- tactile material;
- minimal luxury;
- industrial utility.

## Hero Architecture

- centered promise with one dominant media field;
- asymmetric copy and overlapping focal image;
- editorial split with generous whitespace;
- product interface as the main evidence;
- typographic hero with restrained supporting media;
- compact utility hero for tools and dashboards.

## Section Systems

- alternating editorial spreads;
- modular product demonstrations;
- continuous narrative canvas;
- dense evidence grid;
- stepwise journey;
- quiet gallery;
- comparison or decision structure.

## Signature Elements

Choose one or two that support the product: framed media, annotated product views, layered documents, material cards, oversized type, restrained data visualization, linework, or controlled collage.

The selection should explain the product and tone. Novelty without purpose is not a reason to add an element.
